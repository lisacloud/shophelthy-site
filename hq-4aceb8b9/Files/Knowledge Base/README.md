# Knowledge Base

The thinking behind Helthy, in one place. These notes moved here from our old Notion so HQ is the single source of truth. Notion is being retired, so if you remember something living there, it is here now.

Everything in this folder is a reference, not a task list. Open tasks and the live plan stay on the dashboard (`Helthy_Dashboard.html`). The dates in these notes are the planning estimates we wrote down; when a date matters, the dashboard is the one to trust.

## What's here

- **Product Direction.** How the app should look and feel, the screens we are reshaping, and the products we study.
- **AI & Personalization.** How Helthy turns a scan into advice today, and where that intelligence is heading.
- **Health & Diet Approach.** What "good for this person" means to us, and the encouraging-not-punitive line we hold.
- **What Helthy Does Today.** A plain-language map of every feature already built into the app.
- **iOS App Plan.** How we take the web app to a real iPhone app, plus the Apple Developer account setup (Lisa's first step).
- **Go-to-Market Notes.** Early outreach ideas and the path from first testers to launch.

A few things did not make the trip because they were empty placeholders in Notion (Research, Mockups, Pitch, and a Decisions Log we never filled), or because they live somewhere better now (the old task lists are superseded by the dashboard).
