# AI & Personalization

How Helthy interprets products, meals, and people, and how that intelligence keeps getting better.

## Where we are today

Product recommendations run on a fuzzy-search match against our database. It works as a starting point. It is not yet doing the holistic work we want it to do.

## Where we are heading

Three threads, roughly in priority order.

### 1. Holistic recommendations

Move beyond "this product is good or bad" toward advice that accounts for the user's overall dietary pattern, their recent intake, and the goals they tell us. The score for a single product should reflect fit for this person right now, not nutrient math in isolation.

### 2. A calibration phase

A short onboarding window, roughly one week as a working assumption, where the app learns each user's dietary load and tendencies before it locks in firm recommendations. This makes the first real recommendation feel earned rather than guessed.

Open question: does calibration look meaningfully different for type 1 versus type 2 users? They may need different signals.

### 3. Meal photos, not just barcodes

Let users photograph a plate, get a meal-level estimate, and feed that into the same scoring pipeline. This is the unlock for restaurant meals, home cooking, and anything without a barcode.
