# Go-to-Market Notes

How we reach early users and build trust. Channels, partners, messaging, and the small experiments we run before anything is final.

## Near-term outreach

- **A focused push in relevant Discord communities** (diabetes, CGM users, nutrition-curious audiences). The goal is conversation and early signal, not signups at scale.

## Channels to explore

These are open ground, not blockers. Add thoughts as they come.

- Which channels beyond Discord? Reddit communities, diabetes-focused newsletters, and CGM brand partnerships are all worth testing.
- Who are the credible voices we would want sharing this early?
- What is the single line we lead with when reaching out cold?

## The path from first testers to launch

The rough sequence we sketched for the first version:

1. Finish the full first version of the product.
2. Recruit six to eight early adopters. Lisa's network is the superpower here.
3. Run a pilot test with them.
4. Pick up feedback and fold it in.
5. Stand up a Discord or Reddit group for the early community.
6. Talk to angel investors.
7. Launch in the App Store.

This lines up with the beta track on the dashboard, which holds the current dates and owners.
